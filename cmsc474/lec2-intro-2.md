# What is Game Theory?

# Intro
- Game theory is about interactions between self-interested agents/players who have different goals and preferences
- Even generosity can be modeled via game theory as the result of self-interest
- Fundamentally, game theory is centered on a philosophy of selfishness on the part of agents (players)
- Adversarial learning in AI is underpinned by game theory
    - In adversarial learning, one AI generates and another AI tries to detect whether input is AI generated or human-generated
    - The two AI models constitute different agents playing a game together. The result of this game is that the *generator* AI improves (and sometimes the *detector* does too)

## Algorithm Game Theory
- Algorithm Game Theory is often viewed as "incentive-aware algorithm design"
- Algorithm design often deals with dumb objects, though Algorithmic Game Theory often deals with smart/self-interested objects
- The field combines algorithm design and game theory, and is also known as Mechanism Design
