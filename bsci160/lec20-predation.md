# Predation

## Herbivory

- Most herbivores don't consume the full plant
- Plants are not passive
  - Physical defeses: Spines, fibers, hooks, wood, etc.
  - Chemical defeses: Tains, icotine, caffeie, opium, digitalis, strychnine, etc.
  - Defenses can be energetically costly for the plant
- Plant toxins can be co-opted by predators for defense
- Plants tend to employ multiple defense mechanisms

## Symbioses

- Species iteractions matter because they affect fitness
- Symbioses fall into 4 categories:
  - Mutualism
  - Parasitism
  - Commensalism
  - Amensalism

### Mutualism

- Species benefit other species because acting in their own self-interest happens to benefit others, not out of altruism
- Mutualism can, among other things, improve nutrient uptake for plants, promote nitrogen fixation, and promote plant reproduction via pollination
- Sometimes insects will trick plants to get rewards, and sometimes plants trick insects

### Parasitism

- Extractive relationship where only one party benefits and the other party suffers

### Commensalism

- One party benefits, the other neither benefits nor suffers
