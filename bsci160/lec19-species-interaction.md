# Species Interaction

## Predation

- Predator/prey coexistence can occur if:
  - Predator densities are low
  - Predators primarily eat prey that are close to death anyway
  - Predators switch to different prey when one population of prey significantly decreases
- Many prey suddenly flash bright colors or eyespots to startle predators
- Aposematic coloration: A warning about prey defense to potential predators
  - Could indicate that the prey is poisonous/venomous/tastes bad
- Predators and prey can both use camouflage to avoid detection
- Organisms can mimic other species
  - Batesian mimicry: A harmless, edible species (the mimic) evolves visual similarity to a harmful or unpalateable species (the model)
  - Mullerian: Two (or more) harmful/distasteful species evolve to resemble each other
    - Can lead to a dilution of the learning process for the predator (dilutes the cost for the prey because some of one species are eaten as well as some of the other)
