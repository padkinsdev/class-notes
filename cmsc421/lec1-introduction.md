# Introduction

## Logistics
- The class will focus on GOFAI (good old-fashioned AI), and will be a broad course rather than a deep one
- Class goals include reasoning about problem representation, technique appropriateness, and history of AI
- The majority (~60%) of the final grade will be based on exam scores

## Material
- The disciplinary history of AI can be roughly traced back to the *Entsheidungsproblem*, or decision problem, though narrative representations of and references to artificial intelligence have existed for centuries (or more!)
- One framing of AI is as an amplifier, in that it augments human functioning
- Designing AI is a task of designing rational agents
