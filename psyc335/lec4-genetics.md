# Genetics

### Terms
1. Genotype - genetics
2. Endophenotype - factors (e.g. hormonal sensitivity) bridging genotype and phenotype
3. Phenotype - outward expressions of traits

### Genetic variability
- Teratogenic exposure, stressors, etc. can cause genetic variation
- Parents that create environments for themselves that are conducive to certain types of development will likely also impart those benefits on their children
- Environemtal and genetic factors intersect/interact
- Children also actively participate in their own development, self-selecting certain stimuli (active gene-environemt correlation) or eliciting certain responses from those around them (evocative gene-environment correlation)
- Nearly all traits are polygenic, relying on a variety of genes

### Models
- Diathesis-stress: Some individuals are more vulnerable to the negative effects of adverse experiences
- Vantage-sensitivity: Some individuals are more sensitive to the beneficial effects of positive experiences
- Differential susceptibility: Some people are more vulnerable to botht he positive and negative experiences of beneficial and adverse experiences, respectively

### Twins
- Indetical twins are monozygotic, fraternal twins are dizygotic
- Behavioral genetics is a field concerned with how variation in behavior and development results from gene-environment interactions, and does not generally allow for the identification of specific loci in the genome where genetic risk operates
- Heritability is the proportion of the phenotype in a population that can be accounted for genetic predisposition, or in other words how likely a trait is likely to be passed on between generations (or what percentage of variability in a trait can be accounted for by genetic differences between individuals). Heritability is a population estimate, not an individual estimate
- Notably, the heritability of a trait like IQ is affected by socioeconomic status and access to resources

### Molecular genetics
- Candidate gene studies isolate genes associated with a biological function in animal models and attempt to apply them to human models. They do this by examining main effects
- Statistical genetics is a field that attempts to map genotype to phenotype rather than using animal models
