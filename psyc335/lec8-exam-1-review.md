# Review

- The main purpose of epigenetic marks is to spur cell differentiation
- Methylation, or the addition of a methyl group to the phosphate backbone of DNA, "tightens" the strand, decreasing transcription. Most methylation occurs during gametogenisis and the prenatal period
- Epigenetic methylation largely supports predetermined development processes
- **Fetal learning** - Fetuses learn from prenatal exposure to stimuli (sounds, smells, tastes, patterns) in the last 3 months of pregnancy
- **Fetal programming** - Experiences during the prenatal period affect physiology during adulthood
- Habitutation is the process of decreasing response to a repeated stimulus, e.g. a loud sound in one's periphery
